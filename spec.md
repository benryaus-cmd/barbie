# Project Name

Barbie: Ultimate Dreamland

## Concept

A vibrant, open-world 3D social sandbox where players explore a stylized tropical paradise as a customizable Barbie avatar. The experience focuses on self-expression through fashion, social interaction in themed hubs, and engaging mini-games that celebrate a glamorous, high-energy lifestyle.

## Gameplay

The core loop involves exploring three distinct zones—The Dreamhouse, Glam Beach, and Downtown Fashion Avenue—to participate in activities that earn style points. Players navigate the world using a virtual joystick and touch-drag camera. 

- **Customization**: Access a persistent wardrobe menu to swap outfits and accessories instantly.
- **The Dreamhouse**: A fully explorable multi-story mansion. Each room features unique interactive elements:
    - **Walk-in Closet**: A "Style Studio" where players can preview and save outfit combinations.
    - **Living Room**: A lounge area with a "Mood Light" controller to change the room's color scheme and music track.
    - **Kitchen**: A smoothie-making station where players tap to mix ingredients for style bonuses.
    - **Rooftop Pool**: A slide mini-game where players control Barbie’s descent to collect floating style tokens.
    - **Interactive Furniture**: Added "Smart Mirrors" for instant outfit previews, "Dance Floor Tiles" that trigger unique animations, and "Interactive Pet Beds" to trigger pet-play sequences.
- **Collector Cards**: Eight unique "Barbie Collector Cards" are hidden throughout the game world. Players can explore to find and collect these cards, which are added to a digital album for viewing in full-screen high-resolution detail.
- **Activities**: 
    - **Fashion Runway**: A rhythm-based pose challenge where players hit targets to maximize their style score.
    - **DJ Party**: A tap-to-the-beat mini-game in the Dreamhouse that triggers visual effects and dance animations.
    - **Pet Care**: A nurturing loop at the pet spa involving grooming and dressing up companions.
    - **Exploration**: Free-roaming traversal on foot or via vehicles like the pink convertible and scooter.
- **Progression**: Completing activities earns currency to unlock new boutique items, with performance tracked via the leaderboard.
- **Navigation Improvements**: The camera system features an intelligent collision-avoidance logic to prevent view obstruction in tight indoor spaces like the Dreamhouse. Movement physics include an enhanced "super-jump" capability to allow for vertical exploration and traversal of the multi-story mansion.
- **Developer Utility**: A hidden project file viewer is integrated for maintenance, accessible only via a secret 10-tap sequence on the Settings menu title followed by a password, ensuring no visible impact on the standard user experience. The viewer includes a ZIP export feature for project source code retrieval.

## Visual & Audio

- **Theme**: Ultra-vibrant, cheerful, and high-gloss.
- **Art Style**: Stylized semi-realistic 3D, featuring polished, toy-like textures with high-gloss finishes. Palette: Bold Magenta, Pastel Pink, Shimmering Gold, Sky Blue, and Iridescent White. Lighting: Warm, sunny outdoor illumination with lens flares and sparkling particle effects.
- **Layout**: Portrait-oriented mobile UI. The title screen features a dynamic, high-energy splash page with stylized typography, animated sparkle effects, and thematic character art featuring the specific iconic Barbie portrait provided. The main game UI includes a virtual joystick in the bottom-left, a camera-drag area in the center, and action buttons (Jump, Interact, Wardrobe) in the bottom-right. A persistent HUD at the top displays current style points, location, and the collection status of the eight collector cards.
- **Key Visuals**: The multi-story Dreamhouse with its iconic slide, the neon-accented Fashion Avenue, and the shimmering, reflective surfaces of the Glam Beach boardwalk.
- **Audio Mood**: Upbeat, high-tempo pop music customized for the Dreamland experience, featuring bright, playful sound effects for interactions, UI feedback, and mini-game successes.

## Feedback & Juice

- **Interaction**: Every touch interaction triggers a subtle haptic pulse and a "sparkle" particle effect at the contact point. Interactive items now feature "hover-glow" highlights when the player is in range.
- **Success**: Mini-game milestones are rewarded with confetti bursts, screen-filling UI celebrations, and upbeat "level-up" chimes. Collecting a card triggers a special "discovery" animation and a celebratory sound effect.
- **Movement**: Character movement features smooth acceleration and deceleration, with "bouncy" physics for jumps and vehicle handling to maintain a lighthearted, fun feel. The jump mechanic provides a satisfying, high-arc trajectory to facilitate vertical movement within the Dreamhouse, with added "jump-trail" particles for extra flair.

## Leaderboard

Enabled: Yes. The leaderboard tracks total style points earned across all activities. Submission occurs continuously as the player completes mini-games or unlocks new fashion items, ensuring the ranking reflects the player's overall progress in the Dreamland. The leaderboard is sorted in descending order (highest score first).
