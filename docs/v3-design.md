# Dreamland Golden Hour

Upgrade the existing game in place under the user's broad artistic licence. Preserve saves, Aippy integration and hidden developer tools. The visual direction is an original stylised sunset resort: warm cream architecture, saturated pink details, subtle surfaces, gardens and stronger silhouettes. Existing procedural rigs remain editable; do not claim imported commercial character meshes.

The control priority is steady world-heading movement that reanchors to the screen on deliberate stick turns, with delayed follow and reverse-view freedom; short backward walks and small turns keep the framing. Rendered terrain must match physical ground and the house stairs need a real unobstructed route.

Fashion grows into saved personal sets, salon hair colours/styles and makeup with independently stackable accessories. Old free colours remain free; new premium dyes provide spending goals. Photos always contain the player, support a small group with independent/paired poses and controllable framing. Ken joins the residents. Mochi starts at bed with explicit follow, home and whistle commands.

Activities add a playable five-shot netball session and rebalance drink payouts. DreamFeed follower tiers add a capped style-point multiplier with visible progress. New textures and scenery must retain low-quality support and bounded geometry.

## Approach
Extend the single Three renderer and existing React panels rather than introducing a second game or asset pipeline. Independent workers own camera, style/rig, photo/residents, world/physics, activities and pet panels; the parent integrates the engine contracts, wallet and Aippy release. This preserves the user's working base while allowing actual scene/gameplay upgrades.

## Acceptance
Mobile-sized viewport checks for camera, wardrobe/salon, group photo controls and activities; real Rapier ground/stair tests; model/storage/reward tests; typecheck/lint/build and live pinned import verification. Physical Android frame rate remains a device playtest limitation.
